Number range expander

Paste your numbers, one per line. Single numbers stay as they are. Ranges are expanded into every number, one per line.

How the input is read

A 10-digit line is a single number. A 14-digit line is a range: the first 10 digits are the start, and the last 4 digits replace the end of the start number to give the last number in the range. For example, 78034235763579 becomes 7803423576 to 7803423579. You can also write a range with two full numbers, such as 7803423576-7803423579. Phone-style numbers work too: 780-341-3725 is a single number and 780-341-3725-3730 expands to 780-341-3725 through 780-341-3730. Lines that start with **, or that contain text such as **CRTC MIGRATION TO GOCO, are ignored.
